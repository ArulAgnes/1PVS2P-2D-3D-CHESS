# ARCANE CHESS — ASSET LICENSING POLICY

This project must remain commercially safe. This policy is binding for every asset.

## 1. Only these are allowed

1. **Original assets** — created by us (procedural, hand-authored, or generated with
   tools whose terms grant full commercial rights).
2. **Properly licensed assets** — with a verifiable license permitting commercial,
   redistributable use (e.g., CC0, CC-BY with attribution tracked, or purchased license).
3. **Generated assets** — only when the generator's terms explicitly grant commercial
   use and ownership/transfer of outputs.

## 2. Never allowed

- Harry Potter / Hogwarts / Wizarding-World IP (characters, names, logos, designs).
- Movie stills, rips, models, textures, music, or sound from any film or game.
- "Fair use" assumptions about commercial products.
- Assuming a free download or AI output is automatically commercial-safe.

## 3. Audit requirement

- Every shipped asset must appear in `docs/ASSET_LICENSE_AUDIT.md` with origin, license,
  and rights justification.
- Placeholder/temporary assets must be marked TEMPORARY and have a documented migration path.

## 4. AI generation stance

An AI-generated image/model is not automatically free of rights issues. We will only use
generated assets that (a) do not reproduce recognizable copyrighted characters/art, and
(b) come from tools granting commercial rights to outputs.
